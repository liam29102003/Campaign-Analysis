NOTES ON DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------
- Campaign participation, transaction volume/value, and post-campaign
  retention are generated causally: eligibility rules depend on
  campaign type and customer traits; participation depends on a
  per-campaign participation rate combined with each customer's
  (hidden, persistent) propensity; transaction volume is boosted
  during and after campaigns a customer participated in, with the
  size and persistence of that boost varying by campaign, producing
  a natural spread of over-performing, under-performing, high-cost/
  low-return, and high-quality/low-reach campaigns.
- Customers carry persistent hidden behavioral traits (base
  transaction rate, typical transaction size, campaign
  responsiveness, preferred merchant category) so behavior is
  consistent over time rather than re-randomized per transaction.
- fct_customer_daily_activity is generated over a bounded
  observation window per customer (anchored on their first campaign
  participation date, or a random point in their active lifetime)
  rather than every date for every customer, to keep the grain
  analytically useful (retention windows) without needless volume.
- Campaign targets (target_customer_count/transaction_count/amount)
  are set independently of realized performance, so actual results
  naturally beat, meet, or miss targets.
