**Subject: Request: Previous Year Campaign & Promotion Performance Analysis**

Hi Data Analytics Team,

As we begin the new year, I would like to review the performance of the marketing campaigns and promotions conducted during the previous year.

Please prepare a **Previous Year Campaign & Promotion Performance Report** covering all campaigns and promotions that **started and ended within the previous calendar year**. Please exclude campaigns that started in the previous year but continued into the current year, as well as campaigns that started before the previous year.

The purpose of this analysis is not only to summarize campaign activity, but to help the Marketing team understand **which campaigns created meaningful customer and business impact and which approaches we should continue, improve, or discontinue.**

### 1. Overall Campaign Performance

Please provide:

* Total number of campaigns and promotions conducted
* Total campaign budget and actual campaign/reward cost
* Total customers targeted and participating
* Total transactions generated during campaigns
* Total transaction value generated
* Total cashback/discount/reward provided
* Overall campaign conversion/participation rate
* Overall campaign ROI

### 2. Campaign-by-Campaign Performance

For each campaign, please provide:

* Campaign name and campaign type
* Campaign period
* Campaign objective
* Budget and actual cost
* Target customers and actual participants
* Participation/conversion rate
* Transaction count
* Transaction value
* Average transaction value
* Reward/cashback/discount cost
* New customers acquired
* Cost per acquired customer
* Campaign ROI

Please identify the **top-performing and underperforming campaigns** based on appropriate business metrics.

### 3. Campaign Type Analysis

Please compare performance across different promotion/campaign types, such as:

* Cashback
* Discount
* Voucher
* Referral
* Fee waiver
* Loyalty/reward campaigns
* Merchant-specific promotions
* Other relevant campaign types

For each campaign type, please analyze:

* Average ROI
* Customer participation
* New customer acquisition
* Transaction value
* Campaign cost
* Customer retention after the campaign

The objective is to understand **which types of campaigns consistently produce better results.**

### 4. Customer Segment Analysis

Please analyze campaign performance across relevant customer segments.

I would like to understand:

* Which customer segments participate most?
* Which segments generate the highest transaction value?
* Which segments have the highest conversion rate?
* Which campaigns are most effective for new customers?
* Which campaigns are most effective for existing customers?
* Are we spending disproportionately on customers who would have transacted anyway?

Please include relevant dimensions such as customer type, customer segment, geography, and acquisition channel where available.

### 5. New Customer Acquisition

For campaigns intended to acquire customers, please analyze:

* Number of new customers acquired
* Cost per acquired customer (CAC)
* New customer activation rate
* First-transaction conversion
* Transaction activity after acquisition
* 30/60/90-day retention where data is available

Please identify campaigns that generated **high-volume but low-quality acquisition** versus campaigns that generated fewer but more valuable customers.

### 6. Post-Campaign Customer Behavior

For each major campaign, please investigate what happened after the campaign ended.

Specifically:

* Did participating customers remain active?
* Did their transaction frequency remain higher?
* Did their transaction value remain higher?
* What percentage remained active after 30, 60, and 90 days?
* Which campaigns produced the strongest sustained customer behavior?

### 7. Campaign Effectiveness / Incremental Impact

Where the available data allows, please estimate whether campaigns generated **incremental business activity** rather than simply rewarding transactions that customers would have made anyway.

Please investigate:

* Change in transaction frequency
* Change in transaction value
* Difference between campaign participants and comparable non-participants
* Estimated incremental transaction value
* Estimated incremental customers/activity

Clearly distinguish between **observed campaign transactions** and **estimated incremental impact**.

### 8. Key Findings & Recommendations

Finally, please provide a concise management summary covering:

1. Which campaigns should we repeat?
2. Which campaign types appear most effective?
3. Which campaigns should be reconsidered or discontinued?
4. Which customer segments should we target more heavily?
5. Where are we potentially overspending?
6. What campaign strategies appear to produce sustainable customer behavior rather than short-term transaction spikes?

### Expected Deliverables

Please provide:

**1. Executive Dashboard**

* Overall campaign performance
* Campaign ranking
* Campaign type comparison
* Customer segment performance
* Acquisition and retention
* ROI and campaign cost

**2. Detailed Campaign Dataset**

* One record per campaign/promotion with all major performance metrics

**3. Customer-Level Analytical Dataset**

* Campaign participation, transaction behavior, rewards, and post-campaign activity

**4. Campaign Performance Analysis**

* Key findings, underperforming campaigns, high-performing campaigns, and recommendations

Please use the previous calendar year as the reporting period and ensure that only campaigns that both **started and ended within that year** are included.

Thanks,
Head of Marketing
